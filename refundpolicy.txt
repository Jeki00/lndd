A. Digital Products - One-time Download (DevOps Cheatsheet)
Due to the irrevocable nature of digital products that can be instantly downloaded and copied, the sale of the DevOps Cheatsheet is FINAL. We DO NOT OFFER REFUNDS (Non-Refundable) once the transaction is successful and the download link has been sent to or accessed by you.
B. Digital Products - Course Access (DevOps from Zero)
Access to the DevOps from Zero masterclass is FINAL. We do not issue refunds if our system records that you have logged in, accessed, watched, or downloaded any material within the course platform.
C. Freelance Development Services (Build My Project)
Freelance services will be executed based on the mutually agreed scope of work. Cancellation and refund terms are as follows:
Before Work Begins: If you cancel the project before the research or coding phase begins, you are entitled to a 100% refund.
Work in Progress: If the cancellation occurs while the project is currently being developed, a refund will only be issued on a prorated basis, deducting the percentage of work/milestones already completed by Softactut.
Project Completed: There are NO REFUNDS for projects that have been completed and deployed/delivered. Any discrepancies in the outcome will be resolved strictly through the revision process agreed upon in the initial project contract.

We may approve a refund request for Digital Products only if it meets one of the following conditions:
Duplicate Purchase: You accidentally purchased the exact same product more than once simultaneously.
Major Technical Issues: A critical error in our system prevents you from downloading the file or accessing the class entirely, and our support team is unable to resolve the issue within 3x24 hours after your report.

Note: Refunds strictly cover the Product/Service price. Third-party payment gateway or admin fees (if applicable) may not be refunded.
5. Fraud Prevention (Chargeback Mitigation)
Every transaction made on Softactut logs the buyer's IP Address, timestamps, and server access logs (serving as definitive proof that a file was downloaded or a course was accessed).
If a customer files a Dispute (Chargeback) with their card-issuing bank claiming "item not received" or "unrecognized transaction" while our system logs prove that the file was successfully downloaded or the service was rendered, Softactut will submit these access logs along with this Refund Policy directly to DOKU and the Acquirer Bank to contest and deny the Chargeback. Filing fraudulent Chargebacks will result in a permanent ban from all our services.
